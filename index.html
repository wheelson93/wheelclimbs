Road back to 5.12

Last 5.12 send: — in —. The 5.12s came at the end of seasons packed with 5.11 redpoints at Clear Creek, so the plan rebuilds that base first.

  1. This fallFinish Free Willie and own 5.10. Get back on it in the next session or two — it's already gone clean on toprope. Fill the rest of each day with 5.10b–d onsights to rebuild clipping rhythm and fall comfort.
  2. WinterStrength block. Two indoor days a week of limit bouldering plus a simple hangboard routine, with kettlebell work as the general base. Use sunny days at Clear Creek or the Golden Cliffs for 5.11a/b redpoints.
  3. SpringVolume at 5.11. Several 5.11a–c redpoints in Clear Creek. In 2019 a run of 5.11 redpoints came right before Badgersaurus and Hellbender.
  4. Late spring → summerOne 5.12a project. A steep, bolt-close Clear Creek line in a style already sent before. Siege it on fresh days, two or three goes per day max.
