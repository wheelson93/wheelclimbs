Road back to 5.12

Last 5.12 send: — in —. The 5.12s came at the end of seasons packed with 5.11 redpoints at Clear Creek, so the plan rebuilds that base first.

  1. This fallFinish Free Willie and own 5.10. It's already gone clean on toprope. Fill the rest of each day with 5.10b–d onsights to rebuild clipping rhythm and fall comfort.
  2. DecPrep and test. Easy density hangs and mileage, then the baseline tests (max hang, 2-rep-max pull-up, repeaters) that set training loads.
  3. Dec – MayBase. Max hangs and strength intervals, extended boulders, then 5.11 redpoint volume outside, all in 3-week blocks with a deload week.
  4. Jun – JulBuild, taper, Exum. Fast trad multipitch and big mountain days, one gym day to hold 5.11, then the Exum Ridge.
  5. Aug – OctSecond cycle. Recover, retest, a short strength and power-endurance base, then a build aimed at the project's style.
  6. Oct – NovOne 5.12a project. A steep, bolt-close Clear Creek line in a style already sent before. Siege it on fresh days, two or three goes per day max.
