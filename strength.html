How to log

After a session, open it in Strava and type one exercise per line in the description: name, load, sets × reps. The nightly sync reads it. Edits within a week are picked up too.

hang, 20mm, +10lb, 6x10s
pull-ups, +25lb, 3x5
ring dips, bw, 3x10
OHP, 24kg, 3x5/arm
L-sit, 3x20s
ab wheel, 3x10
side plank, 2x45s/side
single leg RDL, 24kg, 3x8/side
ham curl single, 3x8/side
cossack squat, 16kg, 3x8/side
pancake 25cm
high step pass
  • Load: 24kg, +10lb (added to bodyweight), bw, or -20lb for assistance.
  • Dose: 3x8, 3x20s for holds, 12/10/8 for uneven sets, or a single 8 reps / 45s.
  • Sides: add /side, /leg or /arm.
  • Hangboard: add the edge, like 20mm. Hangs default to seconds.
  • Variants: ab wheel standing, ham curl single.
  • Test week: max hang test 20mm +45lb, 2RM pull-up +50lb, repeater test 24 reps, side split 150cm.
  • Mobility: a distance (pancake 25cm), pass / not yet, or a level (toe touch palms).
  • Anything else, like "bicep curl 12kg 3x10", is still logged and charted.
