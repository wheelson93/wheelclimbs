Heart-rate drift test

Steady 60 min at one effort: if pace (or HR) drifts less than 5%, you were at or below AeT.

How to run the test
  1. Flat loop or treadmill at 1% grade. Rested, not after a hard day.
  2. Warm up 15 min, easing up to the target HR (start ~122 bpm).
  3. Hold the same pace for 60 min — don't chase HR.
  4. Name the Strava activity “Drift test”; the site finds it and scores it overnight.
  5. Under 5% drift → AeT is at least that HR, try 3–5 bpm higher next time. Over 5% → retest 5 bpm lower.
