How it's scored

  • Each measure has an end target and a checkpoint for today. The checkpoint ramps through the year, so "on track" means you're where the plan expects you to be right now, not that you're already done.
  • Exum targets reflect a long alpine day: roughly 7,000 ft from Lupine Meadows to the 13,775 ft summit, 5.7 climbing, and 12+ hours car to car (less per day if you camp at the Lower Saddle).
  • 5.12 targets follow the path that worked before: lots of 5.11 redpoints, stronger fingers, then a project.
  • Data: Strava (hikes, runs, strength logs) and Mountain Project ticks. Long days and vert count hikes, runs, ski tours and other mountain days.
